What leaves your books

One attachment at a time, when you ask. With "Allow AI to read attachments" switched on, a member can press Read with AI on a receipt or bill file of a draft bill (or start a new bill from a file). That sends only that file and a fixed instruction, with storage off, and nothing else: no vendor list, no chart of accounts, no balances. The vendor and account are matched here afterwards. The reply fills a draft bill marked "read by AI — check it"; nothing is saved until the user saves it and nothing posts until they post it. Every call is recorded in Audit history by purpose, without the file or the amounts read.

Tokens and keys are stored encrypted with the server's FB_SECRETS_KEY, which is not part of a backup: back it up separately.
