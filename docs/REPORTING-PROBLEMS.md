# How to report a problem or ask a question

Something not working in FreedomBooks? Have a question, or an idea? We want to hear about it. This page shows you how, step by step. You do not need to be technical.

All FreedomBooks support happens on GitHub, the website where the FreedomBooks project lives. There is no support email.

## 1. Create a free GitHub account

You only do this once.

1. Go to https://github.com/signup.
2. Enter your email address, pick a password and a username, and follow the steps.
3. GitHub sends you a code by email. Enter it to finish.

Your username is public. Your email address is not shown to other people.

## 2. Decide where your message goes

| You want to... | Go here |
| --- | --- |
| Tell us something is broken or wrong | [Report a bug](https://github.com/CyberJody/FreedomBooks/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) |
| Ask for something new | [Request a feature](https://github.com/CyberJody/FreedomBooks/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml) |
| Ask how to do something | [Ask a question](https://github.com/CyberJody/FreedomBooks/discussions/categories/q-a) |
| Share an idea you want to talk through | [Share an idea](https://github.com/CyberJody/FreedomBooks/discussions/categories/ideas) |
| Report a security problem | [Report privately](https://github.com/CyberJody/FreedomBooks/security/policy) (not in public) |

Before you post, use the search box at the top of the page to see if someone already asked. If they did, add your details there.

## 3. Fill in the form

Each link above opens a short form. Answer each box in plain words. For a bug, we most need:

- **What you did.** Each step, starting from the Home screen. For example: "Home > Create invoice, picked a customer, added one line, clicked Save."
- **What you expected** to happen.
- **What happened instead.** If there was an error message, copy it word for word.
- **Your FreedomBooks version and your computer** (Windows or Linux, and which version).

When you are done, click **Create** (for a bug or feature) or **Start discussion** (for a question or idea).

## Keep your private information out

Everything you post on GitHub is public, except security reports.

- Do not post real customer names, amounts, bank details or account numbers.
- Never post passwords, your app lock password, or your company file.
- If you can, show the problem with a test company and made-up data.

## How to attach a screenshot

A screenshot often explains a problem faster than words. First check that it shows no private information.

**Take the screenshot**

- **Windows:** press **Windows key + Shift + S**, then drag a box around the part you want. It is copied, ready to paste.
- **Linux:** press **Print Screen**. On most systems you can then pick an area. The picture is saved in your Pictures folder, in a folder called Screenshots.

**Add it to your report**

- Click in the box where you want it, and paste with **Ctrl + V**.
- Or drag the picture file from your folder into the box.
- Or click the paperclip ("Paste, drop, or click to add files") under the box and choose the file.

Wait until the text in the box changes to a link that starts with `![`. That means the picture has uploaded.

## How to find your FreedomBooks version

In FreedomBooks, click **Report a problem** at the bottom of any screen. That page shows your FreedomBooks version and your operating system, ready to copy into your report. Its **Open the GitHub issue page** link opens the new-issue page in your web browser.

If you built FreedomBooks yourself, also give us the commit: run `git rev-parse --short HEAD` in the project folder.

If you are not sure, say so. That is fine.

## What happens next

Someone from the project will read your report. We may ask you a question, so keep an eye on your email: GitHub sends you a message when someone replies. Please answer when you can. Reports without answers to questions sometimes have to be closed.

FreedomBooks is an early alpha, so we cannot promise a quick fix, but every report helps. Thank you.
