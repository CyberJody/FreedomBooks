## Electron and Chromium

The desktop app runs on **Electron 45.2.0** (MIT, Copyright (c) Electron contributors;
Copyright (c) 2013-2020 GitHub Inc.), which embeds **Chromium**, **Node.js** and
several hundred further components (BSD-3-Clause, MIT, Apache-2.0, LGPL-2.1, MPL,
and others). Electron distributes their notices itself in two files that sit next
to the Electron executable:

* `LICENSE` — Electron's own MIT license
* `LICENSES.chromium.html` — license texts for Chromium, Node.js, V8, FFmpeg and
  every other component compiled into the Electron binary

Both files must ship unmodified in every FreedomBooks installer and in the Flatpak.
They are not reproduced here.
